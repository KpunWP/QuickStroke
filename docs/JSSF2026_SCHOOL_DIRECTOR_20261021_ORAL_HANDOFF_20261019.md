## 4. AI and privacy — safe, compact facts to share with school audience
- Defensible system descriptor: **“QuickStroke: An AI-Assisted Smartphone System for Observing Stroke Warning Signs.”**
- **Face:** on-device ML-powered MediaPipe landmark tracking; left/right facial-motion feature measurement and quality checks.
- **Arm:** phone IMU/motion sensors; orientation/drift rules; **not AI classification, not direct measurement of muscle strength**.
- **Speech:** browser ASR converts a prompted phrase to text; app checks complete phrase/technical readiness; **does not clinically measure slurred speech or speech rate**.
- **Overall result:** provisional **rule-based** interpretation; **no clinically trained stroke diagnosis model**.
- **Privacy by Design:** the JSSF research DB stores **consented bounded/derived numeric and quality data** rather than raw face video, photographs, voice recordings, or raw transcripts. Public use and consented pilot are separate.
- **IMPORTANT ASR caveat:** browser speech recognition **may use external vendor processing**; “all speech/audio stays on-device” and “100% offline AI” are NOT established. Locally executed **Whisper is only a presenter-demo experiment**, not a verified Public mode feature.
- **Mandatory safety line:** “If stroke is suspected, call emergency services immediately. Do not delay care to use the app. A normal-looking result cannot rule out stroke.”

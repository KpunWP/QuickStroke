# QuickStroke JSSF 2026 — Scene 07: The Next Frontier & Public Booth Invitation v1
**Draft 2026-10-19 | Poster Workstream A | No engine changes.**
**Research stage:** Nonclinical feasibility/usability study, NOT a clinically validated stroke screening test.

## Editorial purpose
Close the loop from Scene 01 (stroke recognition and urgent human action) through Scenes 03–05 (feasible smartphone-browser proxy measurements) and Scene 06 (measurement quality/limits). Define verifiable next scientific steps and invite booth visitors to explore a non-diagnostic public prototype safely.

### Recommended hero
**07 / THE NEXT FRONTIER**
**FROM MEASURABLE SIGNALS TO MEANINGFUL ACTION**
*Our next scientific journey*

Three connected steps, not claims of achievement:
1. **VERIFY** — Controlled reference tasks, device variation and test–retest repeatability. E.g. Face tracking against an independent reference and standardized pose; Arm device orientation against calibrated angle/reference; Speech comparison with consented human transcription and well-defined browser/ASR quality checks.
2. **VALIDATE** — Under proper ethics/clinical governance, recruit clinician-assessed patients and controls, establish reference labels, evaluate thresholds and (if scientifically justified) predictive models with independent validation. Do not assume current rules or ASR outputs diagnose facial palsy, arm weakness or dysarthria.
3. **STUDY IMPACT** — Only after clinical validation, investigate whether safe presentation and emergency guidance improves awareness and *actual decisions/time to seek care*; don't imply reduced delay or improved outcomes have already been demonstrated.
